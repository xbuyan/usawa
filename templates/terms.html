1. What Usawa is

Usawa is a software tool that analyses employee and applicant data you upload and produces equity scores, plus AI-written findings and recommendations. It is an analytical aid, not a substitute for professional advice.

2. Not legal advice

Scores and findings are informational. Hiring-funnel and promotion results are measured against the four-fifths rule used in U.S. EEOC guidance; pay-equity and representation-pipeline results use practice-based thresholds, and are labelled as such in the product. Neither is a determination of legal compliance in Kenya or anywhere else. Findings and recommendations written by AI can be wrong. Check anything important with a qualified professional before acting on it.
